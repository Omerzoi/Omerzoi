## Selected Professional Experience 💼

**My experience spans workflow orchestration, healthcare AI integration, ERP modernization, and public-sector systems.** The examples below describe professional experience rather than public portfolio repositories and do not include employer source code or confidential data.

### Orchestration Service (OS) — Visual Workflow Automation

**I created a visual orchestration service for dynamically defining and managing workflows from granular, reusable actions.** It provides a shared orchestration context, so teams can compose workflows instead of reimplementing the same capabilities across separate microservices.

Workflows can be triggered by EventMessage events, APIs, hot folders, or message brokers. Inspired by visual workflow platforms such as Elsa and n8n, OS uses granular, selectable actions and a dedicated orchestration context to support flexible workflow design and centralized management. This approach can reduce duplicated service development and make workflow changes easier to manage consistently.

### Healthcare AI Integration and Data Pipelines

**I have worked on radiology data processing and clinical AI performance monitoring, including the ASSESS-AI platform.** This work was at the American College of Radiology and previously through Motion Recruitment.

My contributions have included full-stack development, event-driven services, microservices integration, and data pipelines supporting AI services and decision-support workflows. This work motivates my interest in connecting AI capabilities with dependable production systems.

### Enterprise Application Modernization

**At SmartCube Tech Services, I led development of a comprehensive ERP and management information system (MIS).** Its modules covered finance, human resource management (HRM), inventory, learning management (LMS), hospital and school management, business intelligence (BI), call-center operations, and external communication. It also featured AI-supported dynamic dashboards and built-in, Zoom-like web-conferencing. Developed by the local team, the conferencing capability used on-premises web communication services, including TURN/coturn and related WebRTC components.

The platform was designed for scalable deployment either as a monolithic ERP or as a distributed, containerized system. I also contributed to legacy application modernization and microservices integration.

This experience shaped my interest in evolving complex systems while balancing maintainability, interoperability, and operational needs.

### Public-Sector Information Systems

**I contributed to public-sector information systems supporting administrative and cross-organization workflows.** Across roles supporting UNDP, USAID-related initiatives, and government organizations, my work included business registration, payroll, election results processing, document management, and cross-organization data exchange.

These experiences strengthened my focus on software that supports consequential workflows and serves diverse users.
